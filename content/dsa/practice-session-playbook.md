# Practice Session Playbook

**For instructors, any practice block.** Same ritual every time — only the clock changes. Read once fully; after that, use the **cheat sheet** at the end.

Text in quotes is what you say out loud, close to verbatim.

---

## 1. What this is

A structured, timed work session — not a lecture, not silent seatwork.

- Students attempt problems themselves.
- You check progress out loud every 15 minutes — ask pod by pod.
- You solve, at the end, only one problem — ask students which one to choose.
- Every student shows one passing output before leaving, if operationally possible.

**The one rule that matters most:** run the periodic check every time — even when the room looks busy — and never let anyone leave without showing you a result.

---

## 2. Before students arrive (5 min)

1. Progress tracker drawn on the board — a simple board works. After every 15-minute checkpoint, note how many problems each pod has solved, in total. Every student works the same problem set, so duplicate counts within a pod are fine — divide the pod's total by its size at the end to get the average per student.
2. Countdown timer visible, running for the whole block.
3. Decide the pod length before you walk in and write checkpoint minutes on the board.

---

## 3. Pods

**Groups of 7–8 seats.** A pod is a block of seats, not a chosen group — if you sit there, you're in that pod. Nobody picks, nobody moves.

Pods needed ≈ class size ÷ 7.5, rounded so no pod exceeds 8:

| Class size | Pods |
|---|---|
| 50 | 7 |
| 70 | 9 |
| 85 | 11 |
| 100 | 13 |

---

## 4. The periodic check ("the ladder")

Run it at the **15-minute mark, the 30-minute mark, the 45-minute mark, and once more at the end of the session** — four checks, every block. Takes 90 seconds each.

> *"Everyone stop. Pod by pod — call out how many problems your pod has solved so far, total."*

Go pod by pod, write each count on the board next to that pod's column, then:

- **Compare to the last check, out loud:** *"Pod C went from six to eleven. Keep going."*
- **Name the sticking point you saw circulating** — the problem, not a student.

**Never skip a check because the room looks busy** — the check is what makes it look busy. If a pod's count goes flat between checks, go stand with that pod.

---

## 5. Launch

Stand at the front. Wait for quiet.

Explain the guidelines — what this block is, that everyone works the same problem set, and the passing-output-before-you-leave rule. Tell them their pod. Tell them:

> *"I'll ask every fifteen minutes how many problems your pod has solved — call it out when I get to you."*

Then:

> *"Go — start now."*

---

## 6. Live solve (~7 min, if time permits)

Ask students which problem to solve:

> *"Pick one — which problem should I solve?"*

- **If time permits:** type it live, from scratch, talking through your thinking. Make a deliberate mistake and read the error aloud — errors are normal, not a sign of failure. Pull the next line from the room: *"What goes on line two?"*
- **If not:** skip the live typing. Just explain the approach — five minutes, step by step, out loud.

---

## 7. Exit gate (~5 min)

> *"Before you leave: one problem that runs, shown to me."*

Stand at the door, visible, not blocking. Then: *"In chat or to your pod, say which problem you're doing first next time."*

Read the board on the way out, citing the real trend: *"Fifty-two of you finished two or more today, up from eleven three weeks ago."*

---

## 8. When something goes off-script

| Situation | Do this |
|---|---|
| Attendance far below normal | Run pods smaller, don't merge. Under ~20 total, skip pods and just do straight show-of-hands checks. |
| Room finishing early | Let them go. End the session. |
| Room behind (numbers flat at a check) | Give the room a hint and the approach to solve the current problem — nothing more. |
| One pod struggling as a group | Go to that pod, talk to them, see what they're stuck on, give hints, tell them the approach. That's all. |

---

## 9. Cheat sheet

| Checkpoint | You are doing |
|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Launch — guidelines, pods, the every-15-min check, then "go" |
| 15-min mark | Check 1 — pod by pod, write totals on board, compare |
| 30-min mark | Check 2 — pod by pod, compare to Check 1 |
| 45-min mark | Check 3 — pod by pod, compare to Check 2 |
| Final ~7 min | Ask which problem to solve — live-solve if time permits, else explain the approach in 5 min |
| End of session | Check 4 — pod by pod, final totals; then exit gate: passing output each, next-session commitment |

**Four checks, every block, no exceptions** — 15-minute mark, 30-minute mark, 45-minute mark, and the end.
